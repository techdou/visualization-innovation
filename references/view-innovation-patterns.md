# View and Visual-Encoding Innovation Patterns

## Relation-as-space
Map influence, semantic relation, overlap, or disagreement into position/distance. Explicitly define what distance means.

## Difference-first
Visualize residual, ratio, delta, or transition directly rather than forcing mental subtraction.

## Evidence-carrying mark
Combine summary with uncertainty, provenance, or sample support without overloading channels.

## Missingness / empty-space
Represent uncovered regions, missing combinations, or low-support zones as actionable objects.

## Constraint / force view
Use constraint-based layout when the relation is better described by forces/containment than Cartesian axes.

## Focus+context
Preserve global structure while expanding a locally relevant region. Explain distortion when precise comparison matters.

## Multi-resolution / semantic zoom
Change representation with scale: aggregate→cluster→sample, community→subgraph, region→profile.

## Hybrid table-visual
Integrate micro-distributions, glyphs, and linked highlights into exact raw evidence rows.

## Temporal state strip
Show analysis/model/story states and deltas as a compact history lane.

## Embedded micro-visualization
Place a tiny visualization at the point where a textual/data claim is read, following the general principle exemplified by GistVis.

## View-as-operator
Interaction with a view defines an analytical transformation: partitions, constraints, anchors, groupings, or data-generation targets.

## Medium-aware overlay
Embed views in images/video/spatial surfaces while respecting synchronization, occlusion, motion, and readability.
