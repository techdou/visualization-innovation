# Frontier Digest: 2025–2026 Visualization Innovation

Historical V2 digest retained as search leads. Publication status, counts and mechanism attributions below were not reverified in this upgrade. Open the primary paper/project before citing any item; unavailable evidence means unverified. Use the ideas as hypotheses, not authoritative proof of novelty.

## Libra / Libra+
CHI 2025 and IEEE VIS/TVCG 2026. Libra+ formalizes interaction through **Trigger–Target–Feedback** and composes parameterized instruments. Lesson: interaction innovation can come from modularity, composition, transfer, and conflict handling.

## UnitoPia
IEEE VIS/TVCG 2026. Derives a contain-and-fill authoring model from 602 examples; users manipulate **marks, container, emitter, force** and differentiable rendering realizes organic layouts. Lesson: invent a small conceptual vocabulary plus a computational layout engine.

## JuicyVIS
IEEE VIS 2026. Treats interaction type, feedback timing, and feedback intensity as a design space. Lesson: post-action feedback can communicate consequence and become a deliberate visualization material.

## GeoNarrative
IEEE VIS 2026. Multi-module spatial stories combine text, maps, charts, layout retrieval, generation, and provenance. Lesson: story coherence is a composition problem across heterogeneous modules; provenance is part of the narrative system.

## SwimComposer
IEEE VIS/TVCG 2026. Coordinates data layers, view modes, transitions, and timeline segments for narrative visualization in motion. Lesson: **view-state changes over time** can become narrative syntax.

## ChartBlender
IEEE TVCG 2026, DOI 10.1109/TVCG.2026.3689056. Synchronizes embedded charts with moving video using tracking and a video-specific design space. Lesson: medium constraints can define new visualization representations.

## DataWeaver
EuroVis/CGF 2025. Supports visualization-to-text and text-to-visualization composition with call-out interactions anchoring narrative facts. Lesson: narrative units should be grounded in specific data evidence.

## DashGuide
EuroVis/CGF 2025. Turns author-performed interactions plus a communication goal into editable step-by-step dashboard tours. Lesson: interaction traces can become narrative scaffolds.

## Data Formulator 2
CHI 2025. Blends GUI and natural language, delegates transformation to AI, and supports iterative/branchable authoring. Lesson: creative intent is often better expressed by mixed interaction than text-only prompting.

## Divisi
CHI 2025. Couples subgroup discovery with a novel **Subgroup Map** exposing overlap and coverage, enabling re-ranking and refinement. Lesson: a novel analytical operation benefits from a bespoke view that exposes its internal relation.

## Amplio
CHI 2025, Best Paper Honorable Mention. Makes sparse/empty regions in semantic embedding space actionable targets for augmentation. Lesson: visualize what is missing, not only what exists.

## GistVis
CHI 2025. Generates word-scale visualizations embedded directly in documents. Lesson: visualization scale and placement can be redesigned around the reader’s workflow.

## Current frontier signal
The 2026 VIS program emphasizes compositional interaction, feedback design, pictorial authoring, spatial interaction grammars, progressive visualization grammars, narrative motion, and preference-steered recommenders. Innovation is shifting from chart taxonomies toward **grammars, authoring, transitions, feedback, provenance, and medium-aware representations**.
