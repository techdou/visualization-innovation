# Evidence-bound story and export

Connect Question → State → Action → Observation → Interpretation → Alternative explanation → Next test. Distinguish observed facts from hypotheses and include counterexamples or null findings when they matter.

For each consequential scene record:
- scene/state ID and parent state;
- data version, transformations, filters and unit of analysis;
- pinned reference and comparison population;
- query/model version or clearly labeled stored-result lookup;
- view specification, order/scales and selection;
- observed values/source records, claim and uncertainty;
- transition to the next state; exploration return point.

Reuse this state for the interactive scene, static Figure and caption. Test that counts, units and claims agree. A caption must not rely on a hidden hover or an undocumented prior state.

Replay: retain seeds and results where stochastic/remote computation cannot be reproduced exactly; mark non-replayable steps. A screenshot is evidence of appearance, not a reproducible calculation.

Static export: at the target column/page size, preserve legends, active filters, reference group, scale and uncertainty. Replace essential motion with before/after panels or a difference view; replace hover details with labels, insets or a companion table. Verify grayscale/non-color cues where necessary.

For instructional material, identify the first action, evidence learners should inspect, a plausible alternative explanation and a check question. Keep exploration branches reversible. Do not force the story to end in the author's preferred finding.
