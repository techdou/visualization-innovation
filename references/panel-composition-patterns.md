# Panel Composition Innovation Patterns

A multi-panel system should have **functional asymmetry**: views do different cognitive work.

## Focal view + evidence satellites
One primary relation view plus smaller raw evidence, uncertainty, explanation, or history panels.

## Overview → relation → evidence
Locate candidates, explain structure, then verify with raw items.

## Parameter space ↔ result space
Manipulate conditions in one view and inspect outcomes in another; support reverse selection when useful.

## Comparison corridor
`baseline | difference | candidate` or `model A | disagreement | model B`; the middle panel carries the comparison.

## Persistent provenance lane
Keep meaningful states visible; select one to restore or compare.

## Nested detail
Reveal details in the context that generated them, rather than disconnected modal pages.

## Cross-scale stack
Macro pattern → meso groups → raw samples/time traces, with coordinated selection through scale.

## Story + exploration hybrid
A guided narrative spine configures real view states while an adjacent workspace supports temporary exploration and return.

## Panel story test
Give every panel one dominant verb: orient, locate, compare, explain, verify, manipulate, remember, narrate. If two panels have the same role and evidence, merge or redesign them.

For important cross-view actions apply `references/coordination-contract.md`: specify typed selection, mapping, population, invariant reference, undo, and async version policy.
