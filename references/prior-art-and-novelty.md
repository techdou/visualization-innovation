# Prior Art and Novelty Discipline

Do not conflate “different from our prototype,” “different from ECharts examples,” “different from one cited paper,” “new combination,” and “new to visualization research.” They require progressively stronger evidence.

## Search protocol for research claims
1. Define the mechanism in neutral terms.
2. Search IEEE VIS/TVCG, EuroVis/CGF, CHI, and relevant domain venues.
3. Search adjacent concepts and synonyms.
4. Look for surveys/design spaces and newest accepted papers.
5. Search public project pages/repos for system precedents.
6. Record closest precedent and exact differentiating dimension.

## Novelty ledger
Record baseline, closest prior art, borrowed mechanism, changed dimension, new combination, expected benefit, required validation, and novelty confidence.

## Safe language
Before broad search: “original for this project,” “custom design direction,” or “potentially novel combination.”
After targeted search: “I did not find a directly matching approach in the searched sources; this remains a candidate novelty claim.”
Report search date, queries, venues/repositories, closest matches, unresolved adjacent mechanisms and coverage limits. Even a broad documented comparison supports a scoped assessment, never proof of global first-ever novelty.
